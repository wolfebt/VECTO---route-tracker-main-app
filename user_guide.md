# VECTO User Guide 🧭

Welcome to VECTO! This guide will walk you through the app's interface and core features so you can effectively track routes, manage jobs, and communicate with your team.

---

## 📱 Navigation & Layout

VECTO is designed to be seamless across both desktop and mobile devices. 

### **On Mobile**
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Use the toggles at the top of the screen to quickly switch between the **Map** view and the **Jobs** list.
- **Swipe Gestures:** You can also swipe left or right across the screen to slide between the map and the sidebar.
- **Home Button:** Tap the "VECTO" logo in the top left to return to the main active jobs list and close any open job details.

### **On Desktop**
- The screen is split into two panels: The **Map** is always visible on the left, and the **Sidebar / Job Details** pane is on the right.

---

## 👥 Roles: Dispatcher vs. Driver

Your experience in VECTO changes depending on your role in the company. 

- **Dispatchers (Admins):** Can see all active drivers on the map, create new jobs, assign drivers to jobs, edit job details, and archive/delete jobs.
- **Drivers:** Can view jobs they are assigned to, see teammates on the same job, log progress updates, and share their live location.

---

## 📍 The Map Area

The Map is your central hub for situational awareness.

- **Driver Tracking:** Active drivers appear as pins on the map. 
  - **Green Pin:** Available
  - **Yellow Pin:** On a trip/Driving
  - **Gray Pin:** Offline
- **Route Lines:** When a job is active, its route is drawn on the map. Dispatchers can change the color of the route line in the Job Details pane.
- **Traffic:** Live traffic data is overlaid automatically.
- **My Location:** Tap the blue arrow icon in the bottom center to recenter the map on your current location.

---

## 🏢 The Sidebar Workspace

The sidebar on the right (or secondary panel on mobile) contains your company controls and job lists.

### **Quick Actions**
- **Company Settings:** Tap your company name at the top to access company settings.
- **Invite Team (QR Code):** Tap the QR code icon to invite new drivers to your company instantly.
- **Share Location:** Tap the **Share Location** button to toggle background tracking on or off. *(A bouncing pin means you are actively sharing your location).*
- **Check Weather:** Tap this to get an instant readout of the current weather and any active alerts at your current location.

### **Job Lists**
- Use the tabs to switch between **Active Jobs** and **Archived** jobs.
- Dispatchers will also see an **Active Drivers** section showing who is online.

---

## 💼 Managing Jobs

When you click on a job from the list, the **Job Details** pane opens.

### **Viewing Information**
Here you can see the job's Origin, Destinations, estimated time of arrival, live weather at the destination, and specific instructions. 
- **Navigate / Street View:** Quick buttons allow you to instantly open Google Maps for turn-by-turn navigation or view the destination in Street View.

### **Action Drawers**
At the bottom of the Job Details pane, you will find four action tabs:

#### 1. 💼 JOB Drawer
- **Assign/Unassign:** Drivers can assign themselves to unassigned jobs.
- **Invite:** Generate a QR code specifically for this job to instantly onboard temporary helpers.
- **Complete Job:** Mark the job as finished once you arrive.

#### 2. 🛡 ADMIN Drawer *(Dispatchers Only)*
- **Edit Job:** Modify destinations, notes, or assigned team members.
- **Archive/Cancel/Delete:** Clean up the job board by managing the job's lifecycle.
- **Request Completion:** Drivers can tap this to notify dispatch that they are done and awaiting approval.

#### 3. 📄 REPORT Drawer
- **Log Progress Update:** Add timestamped notes (e.g., "Arrived at first stop", "Cargo unloaded"). This keeps dispatch informed without needing a phone call.
- **View Job Report:** Generates a comprehensive summary of the job's timeline, route, and logged notes.

#### 4. 💬 CHAT Drawer
- **Real-time Messaging:** Chat directly with your team about this specific job. 
- **Attachments:** You can attach photos (like delivery receipts or issue reports) directly into the chat stream.

---

## ➕ Creating a New Job *(Dispatchers)*

1. Tap the **+ Create New Job** button in the sidebar.
2. Enter the Job Name and Origin.
3. Add one or multiple **Destinations**. 
   - *Tip: Check "Automatically Optimize Route Order" if you want VECTO to figure out the fastest path between multiple stops.*
4. Add Contact info and assign specific team members right from the menu.
5. **Instructions:** You can add specific notes and mark them as **Priority** (which highlights them in red for the drivers). You can even target instructions to specific drivers on the team.
6. Click **Create Job**!
